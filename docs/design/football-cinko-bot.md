# Football Çinko — Botla Oyna

This increment continues `codex/ortak-saha-v3-1` after Football Loto,
Takım Yarışı and Klasik Grid. It changes only the bot Çinko route and its
supporting search/session code; solo and online Çinko retain their controllers.

## Play and scoring

- Preview a 5×5 board, choose the bot level, then explicitly start.
- Choose a footballer, select an orthogonally connected group, and confirm.
  L shapes work. Diagonals do not connect. Removing a bridging selection is
  rejected until its endpoints have been removed.
- A correct cell is +1; an incorrect cell is −1 and reopens after the reveal.
  The submitted player is consumed even if the turn scores zero or below zero.
  Cancelling a selection or passing does not consume a player.
- Passing scores zero and hands control to the bot immediately. Repeated taps
  cannot submit a second move during a reveal or bot turn.
- Both players share the used-player set. The match ends when the board fills
  or when no unclaimed cell has an unused eligible answer. The higher score wins;
  equal scores draw. Cells whose answers are exhausted are visibly locked.
- Easy claims one cell, medium claims up to three connected cells, and hard
  finds the largest available connected group. Ties are random. The same
  canonical answer sets validate the human and the bot. Bot search examines
  the available answers instead of sampling 400 random players.

## Data and identity

`CinkoBotSession` loads a small identity/search catalog and board-specific answer
ID sets from the packaged V4 SQLite database. It does not initialize the legacy
Repository or hydrate every player's career graph. Search includes eligible
players unrelated to the board, so suggestions do not reveal the correct answer.
Aliases and accented names use the existing `SearchService` normalization.
Çinko opts out of another mode's global search index.

The familiar club/country/league pools are retained. Cells without enough answers
are skipped and an initial distinct-answer assignment is required for the complete
board. Incomplete data produces a retry screen rather than a partial grid.
Country matching uses `CountryNames` normalization, including Turkey/Türkiye.
Country means nationality. League means the V4 competition attached to a club in
the player's recorded career; it does not claim a historical season-level league
appearance. Club selection uses exact club IDs and league selection exact names.

## Interface

The page uses the existing Ortak Saha colors, Satoshi/Inter type and shared
buttons. It follows the user's light/dark preference throughout the match.
Cyan marks the human/selection, purple marks the bot, and red marks a wrong
choice. Icons, coordinates, tooltips and accessibility labels supplement color.

The board keeps all five columns and their spatial relationships. Rows grow with
wrapped labels. At narrow widths or larger system text it scrolls horizontally;
a zoom control increases the cell width. The confirmation action stays within
the bottom safe area. Rejected and ambiguous text remains editable.

Last-move feedback includes the footballer, correct/incorrect count and score.
The result retains the board and move history, and offers replay or return to the
rules. Replay resets selections, history, used players and delayed callbacks.
Manual pause, app backgrounding and the exit dialog freeze bot/reveal timers.
Returning from a manual pause still requires an explicit resume.

## Review and verification

Focused controller/widget tests cover loading/retry/disposal, connected selection,
canonical identity, ambiguity, scoring, pass, turn locking, difficulty limits,
full/exhausted-board endings, pause/resume/restart, retained input, back navigation
and responsive light/dark layouts. The bot-menu test now distinguishes migrated
V4 routes from the remaining legacy routes.

The bundled database was queried directly during review. Fifty seeded 5×5 board
combinations each admitted 25 distinct answers. This checks the packaged data;
it is not a device performance measurement.

Run:

```sh
flutter test test/cinko_bot_controller_test.dart test/cinko_bot_page_test.dart
flutter test --dart-define=UPDATE_CINKO_SCREENSHOTS=true test/cinko_bot_page_test.dart
flutter analyze --no-fatal-warnings --no-fatal-infos lib test integration_test
flutter test
```

`.dart_tool/cinko_qa/`. They are not screenshots from a physical device. See the
